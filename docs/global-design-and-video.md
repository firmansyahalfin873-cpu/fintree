# Global design and video direction

## Product design

The interface uses a dark navy canvas (`#020617`), slate surfaces (`#0f172a`), slate borders (`#1e293b`), indigo (`#6366f1`), mint (`#34d399`) and light text (`#f1f5f9`). System sans-serif keeps the product fast and readable; a system monospace face distinguishes source code. Desktop layouts align text left and pair a clear payment story with a visual of the payment flow. On smaller screens the content stacks and navigation remains available.

The artwork is a single payment-path ribbon, rather than an image of a fictional transaction or an invented analytics graph. Real simulation data appears only in the workspace, with its local and temporary nature stated explicitly. Base Sepolia is a test target; the product does not claim that mainnet, every token or OAuth sign-in is already available.
